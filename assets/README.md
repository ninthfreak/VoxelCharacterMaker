# Assets

The game's character assets, all in `lowpoly/`, all generated by the Python in the repository's `lowpoly/` folder.
Regenerate them rather than hand-edit (`lowpoly/README.md` says how).

Units and axes: 24 units to the metre (the old voxel's size); x across (the character's left is +x, mirror plane
x = 12), y depth (the figure faces -y, depth centre y = 12), z up from the floor. `core/figure_axes.gd` converts them
to Godot's.

- `lowpoly/body_{sex}_{build}.json`: the eight bodies (sex: female, male; build: slim, average, heavy, short). Every
  body has the same corners in the same order and the same polygons; each corner lists the parts it follows and in
  what shares, and each polygon its region, part and piece. Also: the parts' parents and pivots, the band heights on
  this body, and how far the head library's heads are moved to sit on its neck.
- `lowpoly/head_{shape}.json`: the five heads (round, boxy, oval, strong, heart). Any head goes on any body. Also: the
  head's rings in full precision and its face (two flat patches where the eyes, brows and mouth are drawn).
- `lowpoly/regions.json`: the region table (SPEC.md 3.4): every band, its rows, its part family, and whether it is a
  one-row trim band.

The voxel bodies, heads and face that were here (`body42_*`, `head42_*`, `face42_open.json`) were removed with the
voxel system (2026-11); the last commit with them is `fa40fa0`.
